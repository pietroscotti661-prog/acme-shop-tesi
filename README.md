# ACME shop

Based on the ACME shop paradigm (GPL-3.0, github.com/acmeshop/ACME_shop). Restyled for a thesis study.

The small-positive-interaction videos in assets/video are AI-generated clips (Google Gemini), cropped to portrait with the audio removed. The captions are shown by spi-popup.js from the text in spi-config.js. The people in them are not real colleagues or customers: this belongs in the debriefing and in the ethics request.
