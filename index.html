  • C2Timed + video Start the Objectifying + small positive interaction: colleagues Same 33 timed orders, with 3 short videos from a colleague, each shown as soon as the order starts, for about 9 seconds (orders 6, 15, 24). Open
  • C3Timed + video Start the Objectifying + small positive interaction: customers Same 33 timed orders, with 3 short videos from a customer, each shown as soon as the order starts, for about 9 seconds (orders 6, 15, 24). Open
