Ring Court Javascript beta testing

Dear friend, in the next twenty minutes you will be asked to test a very basic video game. We'll need the test for a later study. Basically, you'll be our beta tester! You'll have to check if the program has some bugs. To do so, just play with it!

In the game a ball moves inside a ring. You control a paddle that slides around the ring: keep the ball inside the ring and use it to clear the tiles in the middle. Move the paddle with the mouse or with the left and right arrow keys, and press the spacebar or click to launch the ball.

This software is under test thus some levels will be loaded several times and each game can last up to 2 minutes, for a total of 10 trials.
